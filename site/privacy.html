Privacy policy

Tennis CourtCast · last updated November 16, 2026

Tennis CourtCast is a free, non-commercial map of tennis court weather in the Inland Empire. You can use the map without an account. This page explains what happens if you sign in with Google to vote on a court's condition or leave a comment.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name and email address from your Google account, so we know which votes and comments are yours. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, the service that stores them.
  • When you vote: the court and your vote (Great, Okay, Poor or Not playable).
  • When you comment: the court, your comment, and when you posted it.

We don't use tracking cookies, ads or analytics, and the map never asks for your location.

What other people see

Comments are public and show your first name only, the comment and how long ago you posted it. Votes are shown only as totals per court; nobody can see how you voted. Your email address and last name are never shown.

How it's used and shared

Your information is used only to show votes and comments on the map and to let you change or delete your own. We don't sell it or share it with anyone. It's stored with Supabase, and sign-in goes through Google; their privacy policies cover their part.

Deleting your information

  • Delete any of your comments yourself: open the court on the map and tap Delete under your comment. Tap your highlighted vote again to remove it.
  • To delete your account and everything you've posted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page asking for account deletion (don't post your email there). We'll remove your account, votes and comments.

Weather and map data

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, and map data from OpenStreetMap contributors via OpenFreeMap. Loading the map sends ordinary web requests to those services, like any website.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub page.
