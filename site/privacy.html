Privacy policy

Tennis CourtCast · last updated November 24, 2026 (analytics and advertising added)

Tennis CourtCast is a free map of tennis court weather in Southern California. You can use the map without an account. This page explains what happens if you create an account (with Google or with an email and password) to save favorite courts, vote on a court's condition, comment, or report how many courts are open.

What we collect

  • When you create an account: your name and email address. With Google, they come from your Google account; with email, from the sign-up form. If you use a password, it's stored only as a scrambled hash by Supabase, the service that runs sign-in; we can't see it.
  • Your profile: the display name you choose (you can change it any time), your favorite courts, and your text size and play preferences (wind, usual times).
  • When you vote: the court and your vote (Great, Okay, Poor or Not playable).
  • When you comment: the court, your comment, and when you posted it.
  • When you check in at a court: which court you're at, how many courts you said were open, and when.

Without an account

If you don't sign in, your ★ courts, text size, first name and play preferences (wind, usual times) are kept only in this browser's storage on your device. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data removes them. If you later create an account, your ★ courts and preferences are copied into it so they work on your other devices.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (loaded through Google Tag Manager) to count visits and see which features get used. Google sets cookies to tell visits apart and receives standard visit details such as the page address, your browser and device type, and your approximate area from your internet address. We never send it your name, email, precise location or which courts you starred. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracker settings, or with Google's opt-out add-on.

Advertising

CourtCast is free because it shows ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on those visits.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center, and opt out of other vendors' personalized ads at aboutads.info. California residents can also opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for ads with the "Do not sell or share my personal information" choice shown on this site. Ads never receive your account details, precise location or the courts you starred.

Your location

The map only uses your location if you tap At a court? and allow it, or if you allowed it before and leave "ask how many courts are open" switched on in your profile. Your phone works out which court you're at on the phone itself; your coordinates are never sent to us or stored. We only receive "this court, now", and only when you're signed in. Your most recent check-in is deleted automatically after 3 hours; the open-court reports you send are kept so the map can learn when courts are usually busy.

Local weather ("Use my location"): if you tap it and allow location, your browser rounds your position to about 1 km (roughly a neighborhood, not an address) and sends that rounded position directly to Open-Meteo for the forecast and air quality, and to BigDataCloud to look up your city's name. We never receive it. Your browser remembers that you turned it on, so it can show your local weather next time; turn it off by blocking location for this site in your browser.

We don't use tracking cookies, ads or analytics.

What other people see

Comments are public and show your first name only, the comment and how long ago you posted it. Votes are shown only as totals per court; nobody can see how you voted. Open-court reports are shown without any name ("All courts taken · reported 12 min ago"). The map can show how many players are at a court right now, but only when it's 2 or more, so nobody is singled out. Your email address, last name, favorites and check-ins are never shown to anyone.

How it's used and shared

Your information is used only to run these features and to let you change or delete your own. We don't sell it or share it with anyone (analytics, above, never includes it). It's stored with Supabase, and Google sign-in goes through Google; their privacy policies cover their part.

Deleting your information

  • Delete any of your comments yourself: open the court on the map and tap Delete under your comment. Tap your highlighted vote again to remove it. Tap ★ on a court to remove it from your favorites.
  • To delete your account and everything tied to it (profile, votes, comments, favorites and court reports), open your profile and tap Delete account. It happens right away and can't be undone.

Weather and map data

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, and map data from OpenStreetMap contributors via OpenFreeMap. Loading the map sends ordinary web requests to those services, like any website.

Changes and contact
